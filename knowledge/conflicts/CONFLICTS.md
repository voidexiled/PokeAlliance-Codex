# Conflict Registry

## C-001 — Pokémon roster count/scope

- Status: `open`
- Severity: high for coverage, low for architecture.
- Candidate A: the administrator-maintained wiki home reports 530 Pokémon across seven generations, while its all-Pokémon route reports 910 rows including variants (checked 2026-09-09).
- Candidate B: the community wiki Pokédex reports 425 Pokémon, while its repository contains 847 tier/moveset rows (checked 2026-09-09).
- Likely explanations: home-page species/base counts versus variant rows, different update dates, incomplete community coverage or differing inclusion rules.
- Do not conclude: that either roster is wholly correct/incorrect.
- Resolution work: obtain route/data exports, define species/base/form/variant counting rules and compare canonical identities. Do not compare headline counts as though they shared one denominator.

## C-002 — First-party labeling

- Status: `open`
- Candidate A: the wiki footer says documentation is maintained by administrators.
- Candidate B: the main site footer describes PokeAlliance as a community fansite while linking account/download functions.
- Interpretation: these are not necessarily contradictory, but “official” requires a scoped operational definition.
- Resolution work: inspect cross-links, ownership/contact statements and current announcements.

## C-003 — Rule 6 document scope and numbering

- Status: `open_scope_clarification`
- Candidate A: the owner-provided Discord rules transcription includes a Rule 6 allowing use of client/wiki information for community tools and wikis.
- Candidate B: launcher-cached Terms of Service version `1781216239`, last updated 2026-06-11, uses Section 6 for account responsibility and contains no equivalent asset/community-tools section.
- Interpretation: these may be separate documents with independent numbering and effective dates; absence from the Terms of Service does not prove the Discord rule is invalid.
- Do not conclude: blanket redistribution rights for sprites, maps, music, binaries or third-party intellectual property.
- Resolution work: capture the Discord rule's message/channel ID, publication/edit timestamp and author role, then distinguish permission to use information from permission to republish assets.
